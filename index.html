About

Dan Seasonal Services is a local, student-run business providing reliable year-round outdoor property services. From snow removal in winter to lawn mowing and seasonal cleanups, every job is handled with care, consistency, and clear communication.

Our Story

Dan Seasonal Services started as a door-to-door snow shoveling service on cold winter days. I did this in my neighborhood for years before expanding my services and range. What started as a way to stay busy on days off from school became a growing opportunity to learn how to run and manage a business while providing my neighbors with dependable service.
